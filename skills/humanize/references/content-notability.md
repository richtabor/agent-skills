## Avoid Notability Name-Dropping

AI hits readers over the head with claims of notability, listing sources without context.

**AI pattern:**
> Her views have been cited in The New York Times, BBC, Financial Times, and The Hindu. She maintains an active social media presence with over 500,000 followers.

**Human alternative:**
> In a New York Times interview, she argued that AI regulation should focus on outcomes rather than methods.

**Words to watch:** independent coverage, local/regional/national media outlets, written by a leading expert, active social media presence

**Fix:** Keep outlets only when they add concrete context (what was said, when, and why it matters). Don't add publications or numbers that aren't in the input.
