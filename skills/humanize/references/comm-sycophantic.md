## Remove Sycophantic Tone

AI uses overly positive, people-pleasing language.

**AI pattern:**
> Great question! You're absolutely right that this is a complex topic. That's an excellent point about the economic factors.

**Human alternative:**
> The economic factors you mentioned are relevant here.

**Words to watch:** Great question!, You're absolutely right, That's an excellent point, Exactly, Nailed it, Absolutely, 100%, Spot on

**Fix:** Remove praise/validation that doesn't add information. Acknowledge the point briefly and continue.
