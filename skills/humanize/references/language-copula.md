## Use Simple "Is" and "Are"

AI substitutes elaborate constructions for simple copulas (is/are/has).

**AI pattern:**
> Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. The gallery features four separate spaces and boasts over 3,000 square feet.

**Human alternative:**
> Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. The gallery has four rooms totaling 3,000 square feet.

**Words to watch:** serves as, stands as, marks, represents [a], boasts, features, offers [a], functions as
**Fix:** Prefer plain “is/are” unless the fancier verb adds real information.
