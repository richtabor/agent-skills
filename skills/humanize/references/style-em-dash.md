## Thin Clustered Em Dashes

A single em dash (—) is not a reliable AI tell. After 2025–26 model updates, ChatGPT uses fewer dashes than professional writers; only Claude still overuses them. Readers still stereotype the mark, so clustered or formulaic dashes remain a problem.

**Do not:**
- Score a lone em dash as a violation
- Strip dashes from otherwise natural copy
- Sprinkle several dashes into a rewrite (that is the Claude fingerprint)

**Do:**
- Flag two or more em dashes in the same sentence, or a run of dash-asides across consecutive sentences
- Replace surplus dashes with a period, comma, parentheses, or `but`/`while`

**AI pattern (cluster):**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions—not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address—yet this mislabeling continues—even in official documents.

**Human alternative:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions, not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address, yet this mislabeling continues in official documents.

**Fix:** Thin clusters. Leave an occasional well-placed dash if the rest of the sentence is already human. After rewriting, scan for `—` and keep at most one per paragraph unless the genre (fiction, letters) uses them as voice.
