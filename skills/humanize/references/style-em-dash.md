## Avoid Em Dashes

AI uses em dashes (—) more than humans. They also tend to look like “AI voice” in blog and marketing copy.

Treat this as a hard rule: if you're rewriting, don't output em dashes.

**AI pattern:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions—not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address—yet this mislabeling continues—even in official documents.

**Human alternative:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions, not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address, yet this mislabeling continues in official documents.

**Fix:** Replace em dashes with periods, commas, parentheses, or a short standalone sentence. If the dash is doing contrast work, use "but" or "while".

**Self-check:** After rewriting, scan for `—` and remove any that remain.
