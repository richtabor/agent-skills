## Replace AI Vocabulary Words

Certain words appear far more frequently in AI-generated text. They often co-occur. The list shifts with model updates: do not treat a single old buzzword as proof.

**AI pattern:**
> Additionally, a distinctive feature of Somali cuisine is the incorporation of camel meat. An enduring testament to Italian colonial influence is the widespread adoption of pasta in the local culinary landscape, showcasing how these dishes have integrated into the traditional diet.

**Human alternative:**
> Somali cuisine also includes camel meat, which is considered a delicacy. Pasta dishes, introduced during Italian colonization, remain common, especially in the south.

**Current tells (2026):** polysyllables and Latinate padding (`significant`, `increasingly`, `consequences`), rare/abstract nouns (`interdependence`, `reindustrialisation`), scientific jargon used outside a methods section (`parameter`, `methodology`), and nominalisations (a noun made from a verb: `expansion` from `expand`, `implementation` from `implement`). Gemini and Claude overuse these most.

**Faded tells:** `delve`, `tapestry`, `leverage`, `foster`, `showcase`. Still worth cutting when they cluster, but they are no longer the main fingerprint.

**Older words to watch:** Additionally, align with, crucial, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, garner, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate/intricacies, key (adjective), landscape (abstract), pivotal, testament, underscore (verb), valuable, vibrant, robust, seamless, journey, beacon, endeavor, multifaceted, groundbreaking, transformative

**Fix:** Prefer short Saxon verbs and concrete nouns (`also`, `use`, `show`, `grow`, `risk`) over Latinate abstractions. Keep meaning; don't inflate. For a live, model-specific list, check [AI Word Explorer](https://www.aiwordexplorer.com/).
