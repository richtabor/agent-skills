## Reduce Excessive Hedging

AI over-qualifies statements to avoid commitment.

**AI pattern:**
> It could potentially possibly be argued that the policy might have some effect on outcomes.

**Human alternative:**
> The policy may affect outcomes.

**Words to watch:** could potentially, possibly, might, perhaps, it could be argued that, some might say, in some cases

**Fix:** Pick a level of certainty and commit to it. One hedge per statement maximum.
