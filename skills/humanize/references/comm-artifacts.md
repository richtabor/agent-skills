## Remove Communication Artifacts

Text meant as chatbot correspondence gets pasted as content.

**AI pattern:**
> Here is an overview of the French Revolution. I hope this helps! Let me know if you'd like me to expand on any section.

**Human alternative:**
> The French Revolution began in 1789 when financial crisis and food shortages led to widespread unrest.

**Words to watch:** I hope this helps, let me know, feel free to, happy to, would you like (me to)...?, here is a..., here's a..., I can also..., if you'd like

**Fix:** Delete the assistant-y framing (offers, closers, invitations). Keep the actual content.
