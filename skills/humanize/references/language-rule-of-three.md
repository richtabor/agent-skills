## Avoid Rule of Three Overuse

AI forces ideas into groups of three to appear comprehensive.

**AI pattern:**
> The event features keynote sessions, panel discussions, and networking opportunities. Attendees can expect innovation, inspiration, and industry insights.

**Human alternative:**
> The event includes talks and panels. There's also time for informal networking between sessions.

**Pattern to watch:** When you see three comma-separated items, ask: do all three add meaning, or is this padding?

**Fix:** Don’t force a third item. Use two items, or expand only when the third is genuinely necessary.
