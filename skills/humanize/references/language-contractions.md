## Use Contractions When Natural

AI often avoids contractions, which can make otherwise-clean prose feel formal and synthetic.

**AI pattern:**
> Do not skip this step. You cannot recover the data once it is deleted.

**Human alternative:**
> Don’t skip this step. You can’t recover the data once it’s deleted.

**Words to watch:** do not, cannot, will not, it is, that is, there is, we are, you are

**Fix:** In informal/prose contexts, prefer contractions. In legal/academic writing, keep the original form.
