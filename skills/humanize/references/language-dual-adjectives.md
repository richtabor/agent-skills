## Avoid Dual-Adjective Padding

AI pads sentences with paired adjectives that sound polished but vague.

**AI pattern:**
> We built an innovative and comprehensive solution with a dynamic and robust architecture.

**Human alternative:**
> We built a simpler architecture that supports plugins and scales to higher traffic.

**Words to watch:** innovative and comprehensive, dynamic and robust, seamless and intuitive, powerful and flexible, simple and effective, scalable and secure

**Fix:** Keep one adjective or replace both with a concrete detail (what it does, how it works, what changed).
